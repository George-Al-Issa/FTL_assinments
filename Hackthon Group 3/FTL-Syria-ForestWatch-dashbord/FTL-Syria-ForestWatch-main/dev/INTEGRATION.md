# Group 3 integration

The dashboard combines the supplied team datasets into `docs/merged_dataset.csv` and renders the resulting indicators and analysis figures into `docs/index.html`.

Integrated sources:
1. CRU: precipitation and temperature variables.
2. FAOSTAT Land Use: forest land and planted forest.
3. FAOSTAT Land Cover: woody crops and tree-covered areas.
4. FAOSTAT Forest Emissions: forestland net emissions/removals.
5. FAOSTAT Forestry Production and Trade: wood-production items.

The original analysis materials supplied by the team are kept under `notebooks/` and `source_material/`.
